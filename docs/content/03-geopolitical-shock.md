## Tariff Escalation

The presentation documents a rapid escalation of trade tariffs:

**February 1st** -- Trump signs an executive order to impose tariffs on imports:
- **10%** tariff on goods from **China**
- **25%** tariff on goods from **Mexico and Canada**

**April 2nd** -- Trump declared a **10% tariff on all imports**, with additional **country-specific tariffs**, an event dubbed **"Liberation Day"**.

> **Figure:** A photo of Trump at a rally, fists raised, shouting into a microphone with American flags in the background. The tariff details are overlaid as text on the left side of the image.

## S&P 500 Collapse

### S&P 500 Index Since January 2025

> **Figure:** A line chart from Nasdaq (as of April 8, 2025 market close) showing the S&P 500 index trajectory from January through early April 2025. The index started around **5,868.55** in early January, rose to a peak of approximately **6,144.15** in mid-February, then began a sharp decline. Key data points along the trajectory: 5,975.38 (early Jan), 5,842.91 (mid-Jan dip), 6,118.71 (late Jan), 5,994.57 (early Feb), 6,144.15 (mid-Feb peak), 5,521.52 (late Mar), 5,776.65 (brief recovery), 5,670.97, 5,580.94, then a steep plunge to **5,074.08** and finally **4,982.77**. The caption states: "The S&P 500 has experienced significant losses. The benchmark lost 10 percent in two days to end last week, its worst losses since the COVID-19 pandemic, which tanked global markets. Investors fear Trump's high tariff may push most of the world into a recession."

### US Markets Carnage

> **Figure:** An Al Jazeera chart (sourced from Reuters, LSEG, as of market closing April 8, 2025) titled "US markets carnage" showing Tuesday's movement in US markets for S&P 500, NASDAQ, and Dow Jones. All three indices started the day with gains of +3% to +4%, then plummeted throughout the trading session to close deep in the red: S&P 500 at **-0.8%**, NASDAQ at **-1.6%**, and Dow Jones at **-2.2%**. An annotation notes: "Since Trump unveiled his tariffs last Wednesday, the S&P 500 has suffered its deepest loss since the benchmark's creation in the 1950s. The introductory text states: "Trump's punishing tariffs have shaken a global trading order that has persisted for decades, raised fears of recession and wiped trillions of dollars off the value of stocks in the United States and worldwide."
