# European Alternatives for Digital Products

European alternatives for digital services and products -- like cloud services and SaaS products -- exist across a wide range of categories and offer viable paths to reducing dependence on non-EU technology providers.

## European Alternatives Directory

The website **european-alternatives.eu** ([https://european-alternatives.eu](https://european-alternatives.eu)) catalogs European alternatives to mainstream digital products and services. It lists services from companies based in a member state of the European Union (EU), the European Economic Area (EEA), and the European Free Trade Association (EFTA).

### Spotlight: European Web Analytics

The detail panel highlights several European web analytics alternatives:

- **Plausible** (Estonia, EU, EU-hosted, open source) -- A cookie-less and lightweight web analytics service. Because it does not use cookies, there is no need to show a cookie banner. The software is open-source so it is possible to self-host, but they also offer a hosted service.
- **Simple Analytics** (Netherlands, EU, EU-hosted) -- A Dutch web analytics service that focuses on privacy. It is possible to see which tweet from Twitter was responsible for which amount of traffic.
- **Piwik PRO** (Poland, EU, free plan) -- A web analytics service from Poland, with analytics, a tag manager, and a consent manager. The consent manager is a customizable cookie banner that asks users for consent.
- **Visitor Analytics** (Germany, EU, EU-hosted, free plan) -- A web analytics service from Germany. It is cookie-free and supports accurate tracking with heatmaps, and also recording mouse movements/scrolling of individual customers. Also offers polls and surveys.
- **Pirsch** (Germany, EU, EU-hosted, open source) -- A German web analytics service.
- **nilly** (Switzerland, EFTA, EFTA-hosted) -- A Swiss analytics service.

## OVHcloud and the DEEP Platform

> **Figure:** The logos of OVHcloud (dark blue) and DEEP (cyan blue) displayed side by side with a "+" symbol between them, representing their partnership.

**OVHcloud** and **DEEP** have launched a partnership to strengthen European strategic autonomy in cloud services. This collaboration focuses on providing a **sovereign cloud** offering, particularly in Luxembourg.

Referenced resources:
- "Sovereign Cloud: DEEP and OVHcloud launch partnership to strengthen European strategic autonomy"
- "Sovereign Cloud in Luxembourg with DEEP and OVHcloud -- DEEP"

This partnership represents a concrete example of European companies joining forces to offer cloud infrastructure that remains under European jurisdiction and governance, free from extraterritorial legal reach.
