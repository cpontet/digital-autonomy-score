# Geopolitics and Digital Sovereignty: Stakes, Implications, and Opportunities

This is a structured English translation of the presentation **"Geopolitique et souverainete numerique -- enjeux, implications et opportunites"** (~95 slides) by **Cedric Pontet** (CTO at Agile Partner), presented at **Voxxed Days Luxembourg**.

The presentation explores why digital sovereignty matters for Europe today, examines geopolitical and economic pressures, surveys the European dependency landscape, and provides practical tools for technology leaders to assess and act on sovereignty concerns.

## Key Themes

- **Geopolitical context**: How current political events (tariffs, trade wars, cyber operations) make digital sovereignty an urgent concern
- **European dependency**: Europe's reliance on non-EU technology across cloud, chips, AI, and raw materials
- **Regulatory protection**: Existing EU frameworks (GDPR, AI Act, DSA) that already safeguard sovereignty
- **Practical alternatives**: European cloud providers, open-source solutions, and open standards as sovereignty enablers
- **Assessment frameworks**: Concrete tools (sovereignty/portability scales, Wardley Maps, Core Domain Charts, ADRs) for evaluating and prioritizing sovereignty decisions
